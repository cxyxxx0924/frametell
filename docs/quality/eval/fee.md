# A 1% fee over 30 years

Investing. About 60 seconds. Styles: warm-story, then whiteboard.

The figures are compound arithmetic on a constant rate. They are not a forecast, and not a fund.

- Money that grows at 8% a year doubles in about 9 years (72 divided by 8). At 7% it takes about 10.3 years (72 divided by 7).
- 10,000 left for 30 years at 8% becomes 100,627.
- The same 10,000 at 7%, which is 8% with a 1% yearly fee taken out, becomes 76,123.
- The fee is one eighth of the growth rate. The ending balance is smaller by 24,504, close to a quarter of the balance without the fee.

One jar, or one pile, grows. The fee is coins leaving it. The ending holds the two balances, and the fee beside the smaller one. Do not name a product or promise a future return.
