# A few turns in artificial intelligence

History. About 60 seconds. Styles: chalkboard, then warm-story.

The subject, a short history rather than a product reel, is the kind of film asked for in [this post](https://x.com/kloss_xyz/status/2103652674336067876). The events below are the material.

Use only these:

- Summer 1956. A workshop at Dartmouth proposes the name "artificial intelligence".
- May 1997. Deep Blue wins the match against Garry Kasparov.
- 2012. AlexNet wins ImageNet, and deep neural networks become the way people work on images. This note gives no error rate, so the film does not quote one.
- June 2017. The paper "Attention Is All You Need" describes the Transformer.
- 30 November 2022. ChatGPT opens to the public.
