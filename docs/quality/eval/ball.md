# The bat and the ball

Books. About 60 seconds. Styles: whiteboard, then study.

One puzzle from Daniel Kahneman's *Thinking, Fast and Slow*. Shane Frederick reported the same item in the cognitive reflection test (2005).

- A bat and a ball cost 1.10 together.
- The bat costs 1.00 more than the ball.
- The ball costs 0.05 and the bat costs 1.05. The sum is 1.10 and the difference is 1.00.
- The first answer people give is 0.10. The bat would then cost 1.10, and the total would be 1.20, not 1.10.
- The book uses the miss to separate a fast answer from a checked one. This note has no percentage of people who miss it, so the film does not invent one.

Draw the bat and the ball. Cross out 0.10 and land 0.05 on the ball. No faces.
